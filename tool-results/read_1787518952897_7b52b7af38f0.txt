    82→### RB-001: New Incident Creation
    83→
    84→**Trigger**: Alert triage determines incident creation required
    85→
    86→#### Procedure
    87→
    88→```mermaid
    89→flowchart TD
    90→    A[Alert Received] --> B{Triage Decision}
    91→    B -->|False Positive| C[Close Alert]
    92→    B -->|True Positive<br/>Low Risk| D[Log & Monitor]
    93→    B -->|True Positive<br/>Action Required| E[Create Incident]
    94→    E --> F[Assign TATC Code]
    95→    F --> G[Determine Severity]
    96→    G --> H[Assess Subscriber Impact]
    97→    H --> I[Create in System]
    98→    I --> J[Assign Analyst]
    99→    J --> K[Begin Investigation]
   100→```
   101→
   102→#### Steps
   103→
   104→1. **Initial Assessment**
   105→   - Review alert source and initial data
   106→   - Determine if incident criteria met
   107→   - Estimate potential subscriber impact
   108→
   109→2. **Classification**
   110→   ```
   111→   POST /api/incidents
   112→   {
   113→     "action": "create",
   114→     "data": {
   115→       "title": "[SEVERITY] Brief description",
   116→       "description": "Detailed technical description",
   117→       "severity": "CRITICAL|HIGH|MEDIUM|LOW",
   118→       "category": "NETWORK_ATTACK|FRAUD|PROTOCOL_ABUSE|...",
   119→       "subscribersAffected": <estimated_count>,
   120→       "sourceIp": "<if applicable>",
   121→       "msisdn": "<if subscriber involved>",
   122→       "tatcCode": "<auto-assigned or manual>"
   123→     }
   124→   }
   125→   ```
   126→
   127→3. **Initial Documentation**
   128→   - Add all known IOCs (IPs, domains, hashes, MSISDNs)
   129→   - Link related alerts
   130→   - Set initial phase to `DETECTION`
   131→
   132→4. **Notification**
   133→   - P1/P2: Immediate notification to on-call lead
   134→   - Update dashboard with new incident
   135→

   137→- [ ] All required fields populated
   138→- [ ] Severity justification documented
   139→- [ ] Initial subscriber impact assessed

   145→### RB-002: Critical Incident Response (P1)
   146→
   147→**Trigger**: Incident classified as CRITICAL severity
   148→
   149→#### Timeline Targets
   150→| Phase | Target | Maximum |
   151→|-------|--------|---------|
   152→| Acknowledgment | 5 min | 15 min |
   153→| Initial Containment | 30 min | 60 min |
   154→| Stakeholder Notification | 15 min | 30 min |
   155→| War Room Activation | 30 min | 60 min |
   156→| Status Update | Every 30 min | Every 60 min |
   157→
   158→#### Procedure
   159→
   160→**Phase 1: Immediate Actions (0-15 minutes)**
   161→
   162→```bash
   163→# 1. Acknowledge incident immediately
   164→PUT /api/incidents/{id}
   165→{
   166→  "action": "update",
   167→  "data": {
   168→    "status": "IN_PROGRESS",
   169→    "phase": "TRIAGE"
   170→  }
   171→}
   172→
   173→# 2. Send initial notification via SSE
   174→# System auto-emits: incident:status_changed event
   175→```
   176→
   177→**Actions Checklist:**
   178→- [ ] Acknowledge incident in system
   179→- [ ] Notify on-call manager (call + SMS + platform notification)
   180→- [ ] Begin evidence preservation
   181→- [ ] Activate logging for related systems
   182→- [ ] Assess scope (subscribers affected, network segments)
   183→- [ ] Document all actions taken
   184→
   185→**Phase 2: Containment (15-60 minutes)**
   186→
   187→```mermaid
   188→flowchart LR
   189→    A[Assess Scope] --> B{Network Impact?}
   190→    B -->|Yes| C[Coordinate NOC]
   191→    B -->|No| D[Local Containment]
   192→    C --> E[Implement Filters]
   193→    D --> E
   194→    E --> F[Monitor Effectiveness]
   195→    F --> G{Contained?}
   196→    G -->|Yes| H[Eradication Phase]
   197→    G -->|No| I[Escalate Measures]
   198→    I --> F
   199→```
   200→
   201→**Containment Options by Type:**
   202→
   203→| Attack Type | Primary Containment | Secondary Measures |
   204→|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
   205→| DDoS | Upstream filtering, Rate limiting | CDN activation, Anycast |
   206→| SS7 Attack | Firewall rules, GT blocking | Operator coordination |
   207→| SIM Swap Fraud | Account freezes, MSISDN blacklist | KYC verification queue |
   208→| Malware | Network segmentation, IDS rules | Endpoint isolation |
   209→| Data Breach | Access revocation, Credential reset | Forensic imaging |
   210→
   211→**Phase 3: Communication Protocol**
   212→
   213→**Internal Updates (every 30 min):**

   217→Status: {current_status}

   243→### RB-003: SS7/SIGTRAN Attack Response
   244→
   245→**Trigger**: Detection of anomalous SS7 signaling patterns
   246→
   247→#### Background
   248→SS7 (Signaling System No. 7) is the protocol used by telecom networks for call setup, SMS, and other services. Attacks can include location tracking, call interception, and fraud.
   249→
   250→#### Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
   251→
   252→| Attack Type | SS7 Message Pattern | Anomaly Indicators |
   253→|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
   254→| Location Tracking | SRI (Send Routing Info) bursts | >100/min from same OPC |
   255→| Call Interception | PRN (Provide Roaming Number) | Unusual DPC destinations |
   256→| SMS Interception | ForwardInfo IMSI | Targeted high-value MSISDNs |
   257→| Fraud | USSD requests to premium | Revenue-generating patterns |
   258→| DoS | MAP_ERROR floods | Error rate >10% |
   259→
   260→#### Response Procedure
   261→
   262→**Step 1: Verify and Classify**
   263→```sql
   264→-- Query recent SS7 anomalies
   265→SELECT 
   266→  message_type,
   267→  COUNT(*) as count,
   268→  opc,
   269→  dpc,
   270→  global_title as target_msisdn,
   271→  MAX(anomaly_score) as max_score
   272→FROM ss7_messages 
   273→WHERE timestamp >= NOW() - INTERVAL '1 hour'
   274→  AND anomaly_score > 70
   275→GROUP BY message_type, opc, dpc, global_title
   276→HAVING count > 50
   277→ORDER BY max_score DESC;
   278→```
   279→
   280→**Step 2: Assess Impact**
   281→- Identify targeted subscribers (MSISDNs)
   282→- Determine data accessed (location, calls, SMS)
   283→- Calculate financial impact (fraud cases)
   284→- Check for VIP/government targets
   285→
   286→**Step 3: Mitigation**
   287→```bash
   288→# Block malicious OPCs (Originating Point Codes)
   289→# Coordinate with signaling firewall team
   290→
   291→PUT /api/incidents/{id}
   292→{
   293→  "action": "addUpdate",
   294→  "data": {
   295→    "content": "SS7 mitigation initiated:\n- Blocked OPC: {opc}\n- Firewall rule ID: {rule_id}\n- Coordination ticket: {ticket}",
   296→    "isInternal": true
   297→  }
   298→}
   299→```
   300→
   301→**Step 4: Operator Coordination**
   302→For cross-operator attacks, initiate GSMA coordination:
   303→1. Document all evidence (PCAPs, CDRs)
   304→2. Contact peer security team via secure channel
   305→3. Share IOCs through trusted TI platform
   306→4. Coordinate takedown/mitigation timing

   310→### RB-004: SIM Swap Fraud Investigation
   311→
   312→**Trigger**: Detection of unauthorized SIM swap or fraud pattern
   313→
   314→#### Red Flags
   315→- SIM swap request followed by access to banking apps
   316→- Multiple failed authentication then successful swap
   317→- Unusual registration on new device (IMEI change)
   318→- Location mismatch during swap
   319→
   320→#### Investigation Steps
   321→
   322→**1. Timeline Reconstruction**
   323→```sql
   324→-- Get subscriber activity timeline
   325→SELECT 
   326→  event_type,
   327→  event_timestamp,
   328→  source_ip,
   329→  device_imei,
   330→  location_cell_id
   331→FROM subscriber_events 
   332→WHERE msisdn = '{target_msisdn}'
   333→  AND event_timestamp BETWEEN '{start_date}' AND '{end_date}'
   334→ORDER BY event_timestamp;
   335→```
   336→
   337→**2. Verify Legitimacy**
   338→- Contact subscriber via pre-registered alternate method
   339→- Verify identity using security questions/biometrics
   340→- Check store CCTV if physical swap
   341→- Review agent notes if assisted swap
   342→
   343→**3. Account Recovery**
   344→- Freeze accounts if compromise confirmed
   345→- Revert SIM to original (if possible)
   346→- Reset all credentials
   347→- Enable enhanced monitoring
   348→
   349→**4. Evidence Preservation**
   350→```
   351→Evidence Checklist:
   352→□ SIM swap request form
   353→□ Agent authentication records
   354→□ Device IMEI history
   355→□ Location data at time of swap
   356→□ Account access logs post-swap
   357→□ Financial transaction log
   358→□ Customer verification recording
   359→```

   562→#### Dashboard Monitoring Setup
   563→
   564→**Key Metrics to Track:**
   565→1. **Campaign Activity Volume**
   566→   - New IOCs per hour
   567→   - Alerts triggered per hour
   568→   - Subscribers encountering campaign IOCs
   569→
   570→2. **Campaign Reach**
   571→   - Unique affected subscribers
   572→   - Geographic distribution
   573→   - Service types impacted
   574→
   575→3. **Campaign Evolution**
   576→   - New TTPs observed
   577→   - Infrastructure changes
   578→   - Timing patterns
   579→
   580→#### Automated Hunting Queries
   581→
   582→```yaml
   583→# Campaign Monitoring Rules
   584→name: "High-Volume IOC Campaign Detection"
   585→schedule: "*/15 * * * *"  # Every 15 minutes
   586→query: |
   587→  SELECT 
   588→    campaign_id,
   589→    COUNT(new_iocs_last_15m) as ioc_velocity,
   590→    COUNT(alerts_triggered) as alert_velocity,
   591→    COUNT(DISTINCT affected_subscribers) as reach
   592→  FROM campaign_metrics
   593→  WHERE timestamp >= NOW() - INTERVAL '15 minutes'
   594→  GROUP BY campaign_id
   595→  HAVING ioc_velocity > 10 OR alert_velocity > 50 OR reach > 1000
   596→
   597→actions:
   598→  - type: notify_analysts
   599→    channels: [sse, slack, email]
   600→    condition: alert_velocity > 100
   601→  
   602→  - type: auto_contain
   603→    condition: reach > 10000 AND confidence > 90
   604→    approval_required: true
   605→```

   610→
   611→### TP-001: Subscriber Impact Assessment
   612→
   613→When incidents affect subscribers, follow this assessment procedure:
   614→
   615→#### Impact Tiers
   616→| Tier | Subscribers Affected | Action Required |
   617→|------|---------------------|-----------------|
   618→| **MASSIVE** | >1M (5%+) | Emergency protocol, regulatory notification |
   619→| **LARGE** | 100K-1M | Priority response, customer comms prep |
   620→| **MODERATE** | 1K-100K | Standard incident, monitor closely |
   621→| **LIMITED** | <1K | Normal handling, individual outreach if needed |
   622→
   623→#### Assessment Query
   624→```sql
   625→-- Count potentially affected subscribers
   626→WITH affected_subscribers AS (
   627→  -- Define your affected population based on incident type
   628→  SELECT DISTINCT msisdn
   629→  FROM {relevant_table}
   630→  WHERE {incident_criteria}
   631→    AND event_time >= '{incident_start}'
   632→    AND event_time <= '{incident_end}'
   633→)
   634→SELECT 
   635→  COUNT(*) as total_affected,
   636→  COUNT(CASE WHEN subscriber_type = 'PREPAID' THEN 1 END) as prepaid,
   637→  COUNT(CASE WHEN subscriber_type = 'POSTPAID' THEN 1 END) as postpaid,
   638→  COUNT(CASE WHEN is_vip = TRUE THEN 1 END) as vip_count,
   639→  COUNT(CASE WHEN is_corporate = TRUE THEN 1 END) as corporate_count
   640→FROM affected_subscribers s
   641→JOIN subscriber_master m ON s.msisdn = m.msisdn;
   642→```

   880→### B. Useful Queries
   881→
   882→**Active Critical/High Incidents:**
   883→```sql
   884→SELECT * FROM incidents 
   885→WHERE status IN ('NEW', 'IN_PROGRESS', 'ESCALATED')
   886→  AND severity IN ('CRITICAL', 'HIGH')
   887→ORDER BY 
   888→  CASE severity 
   889→    WHEN 'CRITICAL' THEN 1 
   890→    WHEN 'HIGH' THEN 2 
   891→  END,
   892→  created_at ASC;
   893→```
   894→
   895→**Today's Incident Metrics:**
   896→```sql
   897→SELECT 
   898→  COUNT(*) as total_incidents,
   899→  COUNT(CASE WHEN severity = 'CRITICAL' THEN 1 END) as critical,
   900→  COUNT(CASE WHEN status = 'RESOLVED' THEN 1 END) as resolved,
   901→  SUM(subscribers_affected) as total_impact
   902→FROM incidents 
   903→WHERE DATE(created_at) = CURRENT_DATE;
   904→```
